8. Video 4

importance of building a prototype gone down 
Main Importance = How you have build it ? , What is the structure behind your prototype?, Do you know what you have build ?
Building a prototype  : 

	1. Problem Understanding
   
	a. Who is bleeding? (Target user clarity)   
	b. What is the real pain? (Root cause > surface issue)   
	c. Why now? (Urgency & market timing)   
	d. If the problem is weak, the product is weak.   


	2. Solution Breakdown

	a. List 15 possible solutions   
	b. Cut low-impact noise   
	c. Pick top 3 to 4 core levers   
	d. Clarity comes from elimination   


	3. The Core Prototype

	a. Ship the 3 core features only   
	b. Solve 80% of pain  
	c. Test fast. Iterate faster.  
	d. MVP not equal to Half product


	4. Add On & Optimizations

	a. Add features that boost core   
	b. No feature dumping   
	c. Optimize speed, UX, cost   
	d. Enhancement > Expansion


	5. Automations & Final Build  

	a. Automate repetitive systems   
	b. Integrate AI after clarity   
	c. Scale without increasing chaos   
	d. AI amplifies Clarity, magnifies Confusion


	6. The Current Reality  

 	Problem < Solution < Prototype < Add On < Optimize < Automate
